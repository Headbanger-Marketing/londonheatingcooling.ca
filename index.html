Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to the questions London homeowners ask us most.

Do you handle both heating and cooling, or just one?

Yes. Our HVAC contractors provide HVAC services and air conditioning services throughout London and nearby communities.

Do you offer maintenance plans?

We offer comprehensive AC repair in London, Ontario, including diagnostics, component replacement, and maintenance services for central air conditioning and ductless systems to meet your cooling needs.

How quickly can someone come out for a repair?

Our expert technicians provide fast and reliable furnace repair in London, Ontario. We prioritize emergency service calls to ensure your home heating is restored promptly.

How do I know whether to repair or replace my furnace or AC?

Signs like uneven heating/cooling, unusual noises, or rising energy bills may indicate your new heat pump needs service. Our team provides professional repair and installation for optimal comfort.

Can you service the equipment I already have?

Yes! We provide thermostat repair and replacement in London, Ontario, including programmable and smart models to optimize heating and air conditioning performance.

Do you install heat pumps and ductless systems?

We do. Heat pumps and ductless mini-splits give you efficient heating and cooling from one system, and we help you choose, install, and maintain the right setup for your home and budget.

Can you help with air quality and ductwork too?

Yes. Beyond heating and cooling, we handle duct cleaning, ventilation, and smart thermostats, so your whole system works together for cleaner air and steadier comfort.

Are your technicians licensed and insured?

Every technician on our team is fully licensed, insured, and trained. You get professional, accountable work from people who treat your home with respect.

Do you serve areas outside of London, Ontario?

Yes. We serve London and nearby communities including St. Thomas, Strathroy, Dorchester, Komoka, and the surrounding area.

Do you offer 24/7 emergency service?

We do. Heating and cooling problems do not wait for business hours, so we are on call day and night, weekends and holidays included.
