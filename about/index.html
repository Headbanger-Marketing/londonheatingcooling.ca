About Us

London's HVAC Team for the Forest City & Beyond

From drafty century homes in Old North to the newer builds out in Komoka, we keep London and Middlesex County comfortable when the lake-effect snow rolls in and the July humidity settles over the Thames.

Our Story

Built Around London Homes and London Weather

We are a local HVAC company that knows the difference between a century semi in Wortley Village, a post-war bungalow in east London, and a new build in Dorchester and we adjust the work accordingly.

London sits wedged between Lakes Erie and Huron, and that geography shapes everything we do. The lake-effect snow bands that hammer the 401 corridor each winter, the sticky July stretches that strain older air handlers, the shoulder seasons that swing thirty degrees in a week and test heat pumps hard. We plan every install, repair, and tune-up around the conditions your system actually faces here in Middlesex County, not a generic Ontario average.

Our crew handles the full range of residential comfort work: high-efficiency furnace repair and installation, central and ductless AC, heat pump service and replacement, duct cleaning, smart thermostat upgrades, and ductless mini-splits for the many central London homes where running new ductwork simply is not realistic. One team, one phone number, every season.

Our Commitment

How We Approach Every Job

We size every system to the house in front of us, never to a rule of thumb. A 1920s Old North home with original plaster walls and knob-and-tube history heats and cools very differently than an open-concept build in north London, and an honest load calculation is the difference between equipment that fits, runs efficiently, and lasts versus equipment that short-cycles for a decade.

You get the same crew for the late-night furnace repair, the spring AC tune-up, and the eventual replacement. No subcontractor hand-offs, no surprise line items on the invoice, and the same voice on the phone whether you are calling from Strathroy, Aylmer, Lucan, or just down the road on Clarke.
